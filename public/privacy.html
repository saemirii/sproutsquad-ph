🌱 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

SproutSquad ("we," "our," "the app") is a campus marketplace and business-management tool for student entrepreneurs in the Philippines. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Name & email addressAccount creation, sign-in, and identifying you to other users you transact with (e.g. a seller sees a buyer's name on an order).
Profile photo, shop logo & product photosDisplayed on your profile, storefront, and product listings. Uploaded voluntarily by you.
Business data you enterProducts, orders, expenses, coupons, and delivery details you create while running your shop — stored so your Shop OS dashboard and Business Health Score work.
GCash / Maya mobile numberShown to buyers so they can send payment for marketplace orders directly to you. SproutSquad never processes or stores these payments itself — they happen directly between buyer and seller.
Purchase history (Sprout+ subscription)Managed entirely by Apple/Google and RevenueCat to activate your subscription features. We never see or store your card details.
Academy activityLessons completed, quiz scores, and quest progress, to track your learning and gamification progress.
Device/account identifierA unique account ID used internally to link your data across the app. Not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

We do not collect precise location data, and we do not use any of the above for advertising, ad tracking, or sell it to third parties.

The AI Business Coach

When you ask the AI Coach a question, a short summary of your shop's metrics (revenue, margin, recent order/expense counts) and your question text are sent to a business-coaching AI service to generate a response. This request is not used to train any model on your identity, and no photos or customer personal information are included in it.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which stores your account and app data securely.
  • RevenueCat and Apple/Google — process and manage your Sprout+ subscription.
  • Other SproutSquad users — your name, shop info, and order details are visible to the buyer or seller you're transacting with, as needed to complete that transaction.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone, for any purpose.

Your controls

  • Edit your data anytime from your Profile and Shop Settings screens.
  • Delete your account permanently, along with all associated data, from Profile → Delete Account. This cannot be undone.
  • Manage your subscription or request a refund for Sprout+ through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings — we don't handle billing directly.

Children's privacy

SproutSquad is intended for student entrepreneurs and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Data retention & security

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and delete it upon account deletion (see above). Data is stored with industry-standard access controls (row-level security) so only you and the relevant transaction counterpart can see what's needed.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the date at the top of this page if this policy changes materially, and will notify active users of significant changes.
